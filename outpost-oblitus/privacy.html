2. What the app is

Outpost Oblitus is a field-station clock-in narrative with mesh pathing, procedures, and multiple endings. The first ending usually takes 8–12 hours if you read. Progress saves on your device. There is no account.

On iOS the app is packaged with Capacitor: a native shell that shows the game in an embedded WKWebView. Game logic and saves run inside that WebView.

This section is about story licensing, not about personal data. It does not change the v1 practice: local save, no account, no analytics, no ads.

4. Information stored on your device

Outpost Oblitus stores state with WebView localStorage (keys outpost-oblitus-v1 and a backup). This typically includes the current node / room, run flags and career flags, seen rooms and endings, procedure results, UI settings, and an employee number minted on device.

This data stays on your device. v1 does not upload it to a developer server. There is no cloud-save account.

Deleting the app, or clearing WebView data for the app, can reset progress. We cannot restore local-only progress from our side.

5. Information we do not collect (v1)

  • Name, email, or password
  • Precise location
  • Contacts, photos, microphone, or camera for play
  • Advertising or analytics SDKs
  • Advertising Identifier
  • Sale of personal information

6. Internet access

The App Store build loads bundled assets from the app package for core play. The app or OS may still use the network for App Store updates and system services. Core play is intended to work without an account and without talking to an Outpost Oblitus server.

7. Analytics and crash data

No first-party or third-party analytics product is enabled. Apple may collect standard App Store / OS diagnostics governed by Apple’s policies and your device settings.

8. Children

Outpost Oblitus is intended for ages 12 and up (mild dread; not graphic). It is not directed at children under 13 as a data-collecting product. v1 has no chat, ads, or accounts.

9. Retention, security, choices

Local game data remains on your device until you delete the app or clear app/WebView data. Developer-held personal data for core play in v1: none. No method of storage is 100% secure.

  • Play without creating an account.
  • Delete the app to remove local game data from that installation.
  • Limit optional system analytics / crash sharing in iOS Settings.
