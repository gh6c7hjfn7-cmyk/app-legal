2. What the app is

Alison is a personal journal. You can log headache episodes, meals, water, user-typed medications, sleep, and an optional period day. You can optionally sample local surface pressure, humidity, air quality, and pollen. Optional Apple Health read on iPhone folds food, water, and sleep already stored on this iPhone — including overnight Watch sleep that ends in the window and backdated samples of at least 180 days, with Health dates left as the Health dates — into the same journal so they count toward the clinic packet. That is a journal overlay, not diagnosis. Pattern output is observational co-occurrence only. It is not a trigger test, not causality, and not advice. A copyable summary restates what you logged so you can hand it to a clinician. That summary is not a diagnosis.

Alison is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent migraine or any condition. ICHD-3 diagnosis is a clinician act. Guide text is education, not assessment.

On iPhone the app is packaged with Capacitor: a native shell that shows the journal in an embedded WKWebView. Journal logic and saves run inside that WebView. Location uses the system When In Use permission.

The Apple Watch app is a companion on a paired Watch. It can record that an episode started and an intensity from 1 to 10. Those values move to the iPhone over WatchConnectivity. The Watch does not read location, weather, meals, medications, clinic letters, or Apple Health. Health read is iPhone-only.

3. Information stored on your device

Alison stores state with WebView localStorage (key alison-v1). This typically includes:

  • Headache episode rows you enter (intensity, duration, laterality, quality, associated symptoms, abortive text, optional note, time), including Watch start/intensity saves folded into the same book
  • Meal rows (free text and culinary tokens)
  • Water sips (ounces and time)
  • Medication rows (names and doses you type; not a prescription)
  • Sleep hours by calendar day
  • Optional cycle / period days you mark
  • Environment samples (time, coordinates, pressure, 3-hour pressure change, humidity, US AQI, pollen)
  • Simple settings (diet chip, week shuffle seed, dim)

The Diary tab is the on-device log of those rows. This journal data stays on your iPhone. The current build does not upload episode, meal, water, medication, sleep, or cycle rows to a developer server. There is no cloud-save account.

Deleting the app, or clearing WebView data for the app, removes that local journal. In-app Clear data / Erase blanks the book. OOOPS can restore the last erase or last removed line until the next erase. We cannot restore local-only rows from our side.

4. Apple Watch (optional, paired devices only)

If you install the Alison Watch app on a Watch paired to the iPhone that runs Alison, you can mark start and save an intensity (1–10). The Watch sends those values to the iPhone with WatchConnectivity. If the iPhone is unreachable, the Watch queues the save locally until the devices are together again. Watch openings are on-device UI. They are not a second account and not a network login. The Watch does not send journal rows to us. Start and intensity stay on the paired iPhone and Watch.

You can use Alison on iPhone without the Watch. Denying Watch use does not affect the phone journal.

5. Apple Health (optional)

Apple Health (HealthKit) is an optional read on iPhone. If Health cannot connect, the glass says did not connect and the journal still works. If you tap Read Apple Health, Alison may request permission to read (not write): dietary energy and food samples already in Apple Health, water, sleep already on the phone (including overnight Apple Watch sleep that ends in the window even if it started earlier, and backdated samples of at least 180 days; Health dates stay the Health dates), and menstrual flow only if you already mark period days in Alison. Those samples are copied into the on-device journal. Deny still works. The journal still works without Health.

Alison does not write diagnoses, conditions, or medications to Apple Health. It does not log into MyFitnessPal, Cronometer, Lose It, or any food-app account. It does not claim to be a medical device. The Watch does not read Apple Health. Health is iPhone-only. Health samples stay on your iPhone. They are not uploaded to an Alison server.

Usage string: Alison reads food, water, sleep, and (only if you already mark period days) menstrual flow from Apple Health as a journal overlay, not diagnosis. You can deny this and the journal still works.

6. Location (optional)

If you allow Location When In Use, Alison reads your current coordinates to query public environmental feeds:

  • api.open-meteo.com — surface pressure and humidity
  • air-quality-api.open-meteo.com — US AQI and pollen

Those requests include latitude and longitude so the feed can return local values. Coordinates from a sample are also stored in the on-device environment log. Location is not used for ads, tracking, or sharing your position with other people. The usage string on the system prompt is: Alison uses your location only when you tap weather, to sample local pressure, air quality, and pollen. It does not diagnose anything.

If you deny location, or the feed is down, the journal still works. You can still log episodes, meals, water, medications, sleep, and an optional period day. Environment numbers may be blank.

Open-Meteo operates those APIs. Their terms apply to the request they receive. We do not send your journal rows to Open-Meteo.

7. Barcode scan (optional)

If you scan a package, Alison uses the camera only to read a barcode, then looks up ingredients on Open Food Facts. The photo is not saved. Deny still works. You can type the code. Scan is not a diagnosis.

8. Observational pairs

Alison can show what showed up with your migraine days (food, sleep, water, weather, and other rows you logged). That is a diary pattern, not a prediction and not a medical device. Those patterns stay on this phone.

9. Information we do not collect

  • Name, email, or password
  • Contacts, photos, or microphone. Camera is optional and only used when you scan a food barcode. Deny still works. You can type the code instead.
  • Advertising or analytics SDKs
  • Advertising Identifier
  • Sale of personal information
  • Health write (diagnoses, conditions, or medications)
  • Food-app logins (MyFitnessPal, Cronometer, Lose It, or any food-app account)
  • Chat or a social network
  • Episode, meal, water, medication, sleep, or cycle rows off the device

We do not use location except as described in section 6. We do not use WatchConnectivity except as described in section 4. We do not use Apple Health except as described in section 5.

8. Internet access

The App Store build loads bundled assets from the app package for the journal UI. Core logging is intended to work without an account and without talking to an Alison server. The app uses the network only for optional Open-Meteo weather / air-quality requests (if location is allowed) and for App Store updates and system services outside our control. Watch start/intensity uses Apple’s WatchConnectivity between the paired devices. That is not an Alison server.

9. Analytics and crash data

No first-party or third-party analytics product is enabled. Apple may collect standard App Store / OS diagnostics governed by Apple’s policies and your device settings.

10. Children

Alison is not directed at children under 13 as a data-collecting product. It has no chat, ads, or accounts. It is a personal journal, not a toy and not a medical device.

11. Retention, security, choices

Local journal data remains on your iPhone until you delete the app or clear app/WebView data. Watch start/intensity waiting to reach the iPhone stays on the Watch until delivered or Watch app data is cleared. Developer-held journal rows: none. No method of storage is 100% secure.

  • Use the journal without creating an account.
  • Skip the Watch; the iPhone journal still works.
  • Deny location; the journal still works.
  • Deny Apple Health; the journal still works.
  • Delete the app to remove local journal data from that installation.
  • Limit optional system analytics / crash sharing in iOS Settings.
