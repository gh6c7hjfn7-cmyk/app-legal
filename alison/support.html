Support — Alison

Alison is a personal journal. Rows are stored on your device. There is no account, no chat, no ads, and no in-app purchases in v1.

Alison is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent migraine or any condition. Talk to a clinician about symptoms.

How to get help

Use the App Store product page for Alison (Report a Problem / contact the developer). That is the intended support channel until a dedicated public email is published here.

Location and weather

Location is optional and used only to sample local pressure, air quality, and pollen. If you deny location, or the weather feed is down, the journal still works.

Saves

Saves live in the app’s local storage. Deleting the app, or clearing website data for the app, removes the journal. We cannot restore a local-only journal from our side.
