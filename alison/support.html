Support — Alison

Last updated: 2026-08-23T00:45:00Z

Alison is a personal journal. Rows stay on this iPhone. There is no account, no chat, no ads, and no in-app purchases.

Alison is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent migraine or any condition. Talk to a clinician about symptoms.

First open

After OPEN, an empty journal shows What to do. Tap Got it to dismiss. You can open it again from Guide.

  1. If a headache is on, tap Mark start. Finish the form when you can, then Save headache.
  2. Log meals, water, and sleep as they happen. Type numbers; sliders are optional.
  3. Journal is everything you already wrote.
  4. Watch is optional. Same journal.
  5. Apple Health is optional. It reads food, water, and sleep already on the phone. Do not log into MyFitnessPal, Cronometer, or Lose It.
  6. Clinic letter stays closed until 5 episodes, 8 meals, and 7 days. Then copy or print the journal for your doctor. 14 / 28 / 90 / all days. Not a diagnosis.
  7. Alison is a personal journal. Not a medical device.

FAQ

What is Alison? A personal journal. Not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, or prescribe.

How do I log a headache? Tap Mark start when it begins. Save headache when you can. Type how bad (1 to 10) and minutes in the fields next to the sliders.

Where did my entries go? Journal.

How do I print for my doctor? Clinic. Pick 14, 28, 90, or all days. Copy journal for my doctor, or Print journal.

Apple Health says did not connect. Optional. It reads food, water, and sleep already on this phone. The journal still works.

Watch? Optional. Same journal. Mark start and intensity on the paired Watch. Watch does not read Apple Health.

When does Clinic open? 5 episodes, 8 meals, and 7 days. Not a diagnosis.

Where does my data live? On this phone. No account.

Apple Watch

Optional companion on a paired Watch. You can mark start and save intensity 1–10. Those values move to the iPhone. You can skip the Watch; the phone journal still works.

Apple Health

Optional read on iPhone of food, water, and sleep already on the phone. Deny still works. A miss shows did not connect. The journal still works. Alison does not log into food apps and does not write diagnoses to Health.

Location and weather

Location is optional and used only to sample local pressure, air quality, and pollen. If you deny location, or the weather feed is down, the journal still works.

Saves

Saves live in the app’s local storage. Deleting the app, or clearing website data for the app, removes the journal. We cannot restore a local-only journal from our side.
