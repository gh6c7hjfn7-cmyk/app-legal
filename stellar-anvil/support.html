Support — Stellar Anvil

Stellar Anvil is a single-player idle forge game. Progress is stored on your device. There is no account, no chat, and no ads in v1.

How to get help

Use the App Store product page for Stellar Anvil (Report a Problem / contact the developer). That is the intended support channel until a dedicated public email is published here.

Common issues

  • Progress missing: saves live in the app’s local storage. Deleting the app, or clearing website data for the app, resets the forge. We cannot restore a local-only save from our side.
  • Purchases: this build has no in-app purchase. If a future update adds one, Apple’s Restore Purchases flow applies.
  • Privacy: see the Privacy Policy.
