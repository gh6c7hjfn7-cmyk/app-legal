Short version: Stellar Anvil keeps your forge progress on your iPhone or iPad. We do not require an account to play. We do not sell your personal information.

2. What the app is

Stellar Anvil is an idle forge game. You strike the anvil, manage heat and entropy, unlock Marks, read Chronicle entries, and Ignite seasons for Star Ash.

On iOS the app is packaged with Capacitor: a native shell that displays the game in an embedded WKWebView. Game logic and saves run inside that WebView.

3. Information stored on your device

Stellar Anvil stores game state using WebView localStorage. This typically includes resource and upgrade state, heat and entropy progress, unlocked Marks, Chronicle flags, Ignite / season values, and simple UI preferences.

This data stays on your device. v1 does not upload it to a developer server. There is no Stellar Anvil cloud-save account.

Deleting the app, or clearing WebView data for the app, removes that local progress. We cannot restore local-only progress from our side.

4. Information we do not collect (v1)

Unless this policy is updated and the app is changed, Stellar Anvil does not:

  • Require a name, email, or password for core play
  • Collect precise location
  • Access contacts, photos, microphone, or camera for gameplay
  • Run third-party advertising SDKs
  • Run third-party analytics SDKs
  • Use the Advertising Identifier
  • Sell personal information or build advertising profiles

5. In-app purchases

v1 has no in-app purchases. The App Store listing is paid USA $0.99. There is no Foundry Patron product attached to this version.

6. Internet access

The App Store build loads bundled game assets from the app package for core play. It is not a remote website. The app or OS may still use the network for App Store updates and system services outside our control. Core forge play is intended to work without an account and without talking to a Stellar Anvil server.

7. Analytics and crash data

No first-party or third-party analytics product is enabled for gameplay metrics. Apple may collect standard App Store / OS diagnostics (for example crash logs you choose to share, or aggregated performance data in App Store Connect). That collection is governed by Apple’s policies and your device settings.

8. Children

Stellar Anvil is not directed at children under 13 as a social or data-collecting product. v1 does not include chat, ads, or account systems. Progress is stored locally on the device.

9. Retention and security

Local game data remains on your device until you delete the app or clear app/WebView data. Developer-held personal data for core play in v1: none. Data stays in the app sandbox / WebView storage. No method of storage is 100% secure. Local saves can be lost if the device is lost, reset, or storage is cleared.

10. Your choices

  • Play without creating an account.
  • Delete the app to remove local game data from that installation.
  • Limit optional system analytics / crash sharing in iOS Settings.
  • Contact us using the method below.

11. Third parties

v1 does not share game progress with advertisers or data brokers. Apple provides the App Store and device OS.

12. Changes

We may update this policy when data practices change. The effective date at the top will change. Material changes will be reflected in App Store privacy answers as required. If a build ships analytics, accounts, or other plugins, this document and the store labels will be updated with that release.
